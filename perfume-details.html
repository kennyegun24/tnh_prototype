The composition

From first light
to final trace.

Midnight Coast unfolds in three deliberate movements, shifting from bright mineral freshness into a warm, resonant drydown.

01

Top notes

A sharp flash of citrus meets the cool mineral character of sea air.

Bergamot · Grapefruit
Sea salt · Pink pepper
02

Heart notes

Warm spice and smoke surface slowly, giving the fragrance its quiet tension.

Saffron · Incense
Clary sage · Violet leaf
03

Base notes

A long, polished trail of dark woods settles close to the skin.

Oud · Smoked cedar
Amber · Soft musk

Scent profile

Deep, but
never heavy.

Made for warm air and evening light, Midnight Coast balances intensity with space. The mineral opening keeps the woods lucid; the amber base keeps them close.

The wearing ritual

Where it belongs.

Composed to become part of the person wearing it, rather than arriving before them.

01 · Time

After four

The woods bloom as the day cools, carrying easily from late afternoon into night.

02 · Place

Open air

Salt and bergamot stay vivid outdoors, while the deeper notes develop with warmth.

03 · Application

Pulse points

Two to four sprays across neck, wrist and chest. Do not rub the fragrance into skin.

04 · Mood

Quiet presence

Best when the aim is to be remembered without asking for the room's attention.
